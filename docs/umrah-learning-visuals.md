# Umrah learning visuals

Learn expands each stage into its existing topics on the same screen. Selecting a topic opens the existing reader directly. The overview route remains available for older links. Learning reviews, real ritual completion and practice counters retain their separate storage.

All images are local static assets, bundled for offline reading. The illustration thumbnails preserve the whole image. External source links require an internet connection.

## Guidance references

- The current source audit and the **pending independent scholar review** are documented in [religious-content-review.md](religious-content-review.md). Page-specific references are displayed in lessons; the original Ibn Baz booklet can now be read offline inside Qasd. Source checking is not scholar approval.

### Ihram prompt

Use case: scientific-educational. Asset type: original educational illustration for a mobile Umrah learning app. Primary request: a warm, polished, flat editorial illustration showing TWO adult pilgrims standing naturally side by side, a man on the left and a woman on the right, both shown full length, teaching appropriate general Ihram clothing. Man wears two plain white unstitched cloths, secure waist wrap covering navel to below knees and an upper cloth covering BOTH shoulders and torso; simple sandals. Do not expose his right shoulder, this image is general preparation, not Tawaf. Woman wears a loose opaque sage green full length dress/abaya and a matching hijab covering hair, neck and chest, face and hands visible, no niqab and no gloves, clothing falling to cover ankles. Respectful anonymous simplified faces without detailed facial features, natural warm brown skin, varied adult body shapes. Woman equally prominent in size and visual weight. Style: elegant original contemporary editorial illustration with gentle paper grain and soft dimensional folds, no outlines or cartoon exaggeration. Backdrop: warm ivory #eee7d8 with a very subtle pale sage organic shape, ample breathing room. Composition: wide landscape 3:2, both complete bodies comfortably inside frame, heads at same height, no cropping. Palette forest green #263c36, muted sage, ivory and warm sand, gold sparingly. No text, no lettering, no logos, no watermarks, no diagrams, no square icon tiles. This is original artwork inspired by the general idea of an Ihram teaching illustration, do not copy any stock illustration.

## Additional illustration prompts

All three final assets below were created with the built-in image_gen tool, then resized/exported as JPEG for offline bundling. The first male shaving variant was discarded because it resembled a haircut with only the sides shaved.

### Men's example — initial prompt

Use case: scientific-educational. Asset type: original illustration for a men's Umrah hair-cutting lesson in a mobile app. Primary request: a respectful close-up side/rear three-quarter view of an adult brown-skinned male pilgrim seated at a barber having his head shaved with electric clippers by a barber's hand. Show only the pilgrim's head, neck and shoulders and the barber's hand. He has short dark hair with one area shaved short by the clippers. His shoulders and torso are covered by plain white cloth; no bare chest, no face details, no blood, no scalp injury. Depict head hair only, never shaving a beard. Style: elegant contemporary editorial illustration with gentle dimensional shading and fine paper grain; match a premium Umrah guide with warm ivory #eee7d8, muted sage green #829379, forest green #263c36, warm brown skin. Background ivory with a very subtle pale sage shape. Composition square 1:1, clear subject large enough for a small educational card, all critical clipper and scalp details within central 80 percent. No text, no labels, no logos, no watermark.

### Men's example — final corrective edit

Edit this educational men's Umrah head-shaving illustration. Keep the same hand with electric clippers, same brown-skinned adult pilgrim, same white shoulder covering, camera angle, square composition, gentle paper-grain editorial style and ivory/sage background. Change ONLY the hair: the whole head should be almost completely shaved to uniform very short stubble, with just a small remaining patch being removed by the clippers at the upper back of the head. Remove the long curly top entirely. This must look like shaving the whole head for Umrah, not a fashionable fade or shaving just the sides. Keep the beard untouched. No text, logos, injury or blood.

### Raised hands in Makkah

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: original completion image for an Umrah learning lesson. Primary request: first-person view of two warm brown adult hands raised and gently cupped in du’a in the foreground, palms facing upward in a natural relaxed prayer pose, wearing simple modest ivory sleeves, with the Ka’bah recognizable in the middle distance inside Masjid al-Haram in Makkah. The Ka’bah has its familiar black kiswah and one gold band, and the surrounding pale mosque arcades are softly atmospheric. Two hands only, anatomically correct, no fingers missing or extra. Mood peaceful, grateful, reverent, soft late-afternoon light. Style: refined editorial illustration with soft lifelike forms, subtle paper grain, warm ivory and pale stone, muted sage and forest green accents, restrained gold. Clearly an artwork, not a documentary photograph. Composition landscape 3:2, hands in lower half, Ka’bah in upper center, all hands comfortably in frame. No text, no logos, no watermarks, no prayer beads, no invented directional signs. This image conveys gratitude after learning about Umrah, not a required final ritual.

### Green marker recognition

Use case: scientific-educational. Asset type: original marker-recognition illustration for the Tawaf start lesson of an Umrah learning app. Primary request: a clear eye-level teaching illustration of the green vertical illuminated Tawaf start-line marker on a pale marble wall/column of the covered colonnade facing the Ka’bah inside Masjid al-Haram. One bright vivid green vertical LED light strip fixed above head height, mounted on the pale marble column between simple pale stone arches, lit clearly against an otherwise calm ivory and sage background. The strip is the dominant subject, recognizable at thumbnail size. No Ka’bah, no minarets, no people, no floor line, no exit or traffic light, no running lane, no arrow signs, no lettering or Arabic text. Show just enough of the mosque’s marble wall and arches to establish the setting. Original elegant editorial illustration with softly realistic architectural forms, delicate paper grain and warm ivory #eee7d8 plus muted sage; the light itself saturated green. Square 1:1 composition. Clearly artwork rather than a claimed documentary photograph. No text, logos or watermarks. This will be explicitly labelled as a marker illustration and paired with a separate alignment diagram.
